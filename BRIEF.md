## 0. Evidence vs. Project Vision & Authored Decisions

### Core Project Vision & Philosophy
- **Foundational Motto:** **`命好不如习惯好` — Good habits matter more than good fortune.**
- **Role of Technology:** *"Technology is only an enabler. Good habits create lasting success. Programming is simply one tool that helps children develop positive thinking, discipline, creativity, perseverance, compassion, and responsibility."*
- **The Three Loves (`三愛` / Three Core Values):**
  1. **Love Yourself (`爱自己`):** Positive thinking, discipline, patience while debugging, and self-stewardship.
  2. **Love Others (`爱别人`):** Compassion, empathy, teamwork, and using Python to brighten neighbors' lives alongside `小慈` care-bots.
  3. **Love the Earth (`爱地球`):** Environmental responsibility, clean watershed care, and sustainable engineering.
- **Dual-Harvest Pedagogy:** Every lesson ensures students leave having learned **both a Python concept and a positive life habit**, empowering them to become kind, responsible problem-solvers.
- **Role of the Landing Page:** *"The landing page is the front gate into Kindness City. Students are not simply opening a website. They are beginning an adventure. Kindness City should feel alive. Technology and nature coexist. Programming helps improve the city. Acts of kindness make the city flourish. Every lesson contributes to making Kindness City a better place."*
